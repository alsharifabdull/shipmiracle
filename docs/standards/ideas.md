# Feature ideas (on demand)

- Ideas for later live in the project's `docs/FEATURE-IDEAS.md` (the "Ideas" tab of the Shipmiracle panel), edited with `shipm idea` (prefer it to hand edits).
- "Add idea: ..." appends an item; "what ideas are open?" lists them; "is F3 worth it?" follows `docs/idea-review-method.md` in the Shipmiracle engine folder, in plan mode (one page per idea, a verdict, wait for approval).
- An idea with no clear home goes to the Inbox (`shipm idea -p ideas add ...`).
- Never build an idea before its plan is approved.
