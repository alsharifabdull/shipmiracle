# Code of conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer. The rules are short.

## What we expect

- Be kind and be specific. A good report says what you did, what you saw and what you expected.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep private things private: never post secrets, tokens, passwords, or logs that show personal paths or other people's data.
- Stay on topic. Issues are for bugs and ideas about this project.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind, or sexual content.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or pushing the maintainer to act faster than the project allows.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules, without warning when the
case is clear. These rules apply in issues and in any other space where someone speaks for this project.

## Reporting a problem

Use GitHub's "Report content" button on the comment. If the problem is about the maintainer, or you cannot use that button, open a
private report through the Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") and write "conduct" in the title. See `SECURITY.md`.
